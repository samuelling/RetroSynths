▬About Blasta

    

      
Blasta plays General MIDI through four simulated generations of PC sound card. Drag the slider to age a song forward from AdLib FM to Sound Blaster Live! — neighbouring cards crossfade.

      
Each card has its own voices and instruments: the FM cards play an OPL patch bank you can edit operator by operator (2-op, OP2 double voices and true OPL3 4-op); the wavetable cards play the loaded SoundFont's own presets, with your generator edits layered on top. The Arranger shows where each MIDI part lands — leave it on Auto or pick any instrument and voice.

      
FM eras run a cycle-accurate YMF262 emulation (a JavaScript port of Nuked OPL3 by Nuke.YKT, LGPL-2.1) at the chip's native 49,716 Hz, programmed through its registers like a period driver and fed through a floating-point DAC model, with an original patch bank. Load your own .op2 bank (e.g. the free DMXOPL).

      
Wavetable eras use GeneralUser GS by S. Christian Collins (bundled locally under its license), burned down to an AWE32-ROM shape or an early-Live! shape. Load your own .sf2 into either slot — untick the budget box if it is already a period bank.

      
Banks you load are saved in this browser and come back next time. Default forgets them. Unlock your Bank Locker (a private Cloudflare store, see locker/README.md) and they're also uploaded behind your password and follow you to every device.

      
Drop .mid, .sf2 or .op2 files anywhere on the window.

      
No Creative, E-mu, Yamaha or Microsoft data is included. Sound Blaster, AWE32 and Live! are trademarks of Creative Technology; names are used only to describe the eras being evoked.
