About Console

    

      
Console plays music through the sound hardware of five game consoles: the NES (2A03), Super Nintendo (SPC700 + S-DSP), Sega Genesis (YM2612 + SN76489), TurboGrafx-16 (HuC6280) and the original Game Boy.

      
Each machine has its own voices and instruments. Pick or edit an instrument and play it on the keyboard (QWERTY, on-screen or Web MIDI). General MIDI files play too: the Arranger shows how each MIDI channel lands on the chip — leave it on Auto or choose any native instrument and voice. Each machine runs an emulation of its own sound chip at the chip's native clock, driven by a period-style music driver that squeezes General MIDI into the hardware's few voices — the way game composers had to.

      
Emulations are written from public hardware documentation (or ported from permissively licensed emulators, see LICENSES). No game, ROM or console sample data is included. Machine names are trademarks of their owners, used only to describe the hardware being emulated.
